Five years ago, a nearby Gamma-Ray Burst combined with an abnormal surge in solar luminosity and irreversibly shattered Earth's atmosphere. With UV radiation scorching the surface, sea levels rising, and ecosystems collapsing, humanity was given a final deadline: eleven months to live.

As the planetary collapse escalated, the UN-backed Planetary Defense Force (PDF) seized control of all global communications. Behind closed doors, world leaders funneled critical survival resources into "The Tree of Life" — a secretive project building massive Eden ark ships to transport a chosen elite to Alpha Centauri, leaving the rest of humanity behind.

You play as an operator at a state-controlled radio station. Using covert AI manipulation software installed by the Hades resistance, you have the power to alter news broadcasts before they hit the airwaves.

### Expose the Truth

Leak information about the PDF's corruption and help the resistance locate and destroy the Eden ships. If humanity dies, everyone dies together.

### Play by the Rules

Obey state mandates, appease the government, and secure a guaranteed ticket on an Eden vessel to escape the doomed planet.

### Maintain the Balance

Avoid drawing suspicion from the state while keeping the resistance from deciding you're an enemy worth eliminating.

### Controls

* **W / A / S / D:** Move Character
* **Mouse:** Camera Control
* **E / Enter:** Interact / Confirm Selection
* **P:** Pause Game
* **F10:** Open Graphics Settings
